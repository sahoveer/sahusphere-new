## 2025-02-12 - Missing ARIA Labels and Roles on Interactive Elements

**Learning:** Missing ARIA labels and roles on interactive configuration toggles, expandable menus, and icon-only buttons across Sahu Sphere's custom components.
**Action:** Always add ARIA labels to icon-only buttons, use `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` for expandable menus, and add `role="switch"` and `aria-checked` to visual toggles.
