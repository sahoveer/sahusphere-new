## 2025-02-23 - Accessibility semantics for icon buttons and custom toggles
**Learning:** `data-tip` attributes provide visual tooltips but do not substitute for semantic `aria-label` attributes on icon-only buttons for screen readers. Furthermore, custom visual toggle components (like rounded pills) must explicitly use `role="switch"` and `aria-checked` to be understood semantically by assistive technology, along with `aria-labelledby` or `aria-label` to provide the switch's name. Accordion buttons also need `aria-expanded` and `aria-controls` to announce their state properly.
**Action:** Always add `aria-label` to icon-only interactive elements alongside any custom tooltip logic. When building custom toggle switches or accordions, ensure the correct ARIA roles and states are explicitly bound to the DOM elements.
