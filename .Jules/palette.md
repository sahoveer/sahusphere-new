## 2026-09-24 - Interactive Elements Accessibility\n**Learning:** Collapsible sections and dropdown menus in this application's components frequently lack the necessary `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, and `id` linkages required for screen readers to properly interpret their state and relationship. Additionally, icon-only action buttons within complex components (like the image grid) often miss `aria-label`s.\n**Action:** When adding or modifying interactive UI components with togglable states or icon-only buttons, systematically verify and include standard ARIA attributes (e.g., `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls`, `aria-haspopup`, `aria-label`) to ensure baseline accessibility compliance.
